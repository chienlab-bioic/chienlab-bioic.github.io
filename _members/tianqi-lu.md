Tianqi Lu (Member, IEEE) received the B.Sc. degree in physics from Nanchang University, Nanchang, China, in 2018, the M.Sc. degree in integrated circuit engineering from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2021, and the Ph.D. degree in microelectronics from Delft University of Technology, Delft, The Netherlands, in 2026. He is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher with the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS), University of California, Berkeley, CA, USA. His research interests include analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits for biomedical applications, biosensors, wearable and implantable devices, and next-generation power-management circuits. He received the ISSCC Student Research Preview Poster Award in 2026 and an ISSCC Student Travel Grant in 2025. He serves as a reviewer for several IEEE journals and conferences, including the IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Circuits and Systems, IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I: Regular Papers, and IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics.
