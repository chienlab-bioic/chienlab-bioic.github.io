Cheng-Jui (Ricky) Cheng received his B.S. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University. He is currently pursuing his Ph.D. in EECS at UC Berkeley. His research focuses on optical interconnection and high-speed mixed-signal circuits, with previous industry experience through internships at AUO and Texas Instruments.
